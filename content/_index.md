A registry of insect image datasets for benchmarking computer vision models.

Each entry records what the dataset is actually for: the tasks its annotations
support, the insects it covers, the backgrounds its images were taken against,
and the limitations its authors documented. Data stays with its authors, this
catalogue points at it and never redistributes it.
